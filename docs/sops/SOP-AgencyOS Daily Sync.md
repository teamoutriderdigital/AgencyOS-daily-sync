# SOP — Daily Sync (Standup)

**Purpose:** A fast, standing check-in that keeps every client moving, surfaces blockers the same day, and keeps the to-do list honest. It is the daily counterpart to the weekly L10 — same data, stripped to what changes every day.

**Cadence:** Every working day
**Time-box:** 30 minutes (hard stop)
**Format:** Standing / camera-on. No deep problem-solving — park big issues for the weekly L10.
**Attendees:** Whole delivery team
**Facilitator:** Rotating, or a fixed owner
**Board:** `…/daily` (defaults to today; date picker to view past days)

---

## Agenda (30 min)

| # | Section | Time | Goal |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Check-in | 3 min | Presence + one-line status per person |
| 2 | Client headlines | 13 min | Every client's focus for the day; mark progress; flag slips |
| 3 | Items to review for the day | 6 min | Work through the day's shared checklist |
| 4 | To-dos | 8 min | Clear/assign action items; catch anything due today or overdue |

---

## How to run each section

### 1. Check-in (3 min)
- Each person sets their **status** (Present / Out) and types a **one-liner** ("heads-down on the SBD build", "out this afternoon").
- Only edit your own row. No discussion — it's a presence + headspace read.

### 2. Client headlines (13 min)
This is the heart of the daily. One entry per client: a short update, broken into **bullet tasks**, each with a **responsible owner** and a **done checkbox**.
- Go client by client. The owner gives a 10–15s update.
- **Tick off** tasks that are done (the box saves instantly).
- **Add a task** ("+ Add a task…") for anything new that came up.
- **Reassign** a bullet by changing its owner dropdown.
- Call out **slips explicitly** — if a task is past due, say so and set the next step. Don't debate the fix here; if it needs real problem-solving, add it to IDS for the weekly L10.
- New client update? Pick the client chip at the top, type the update (one line per bullet), **Add**.

### 3. Items to review for the day (6 min)
- A shared per-day checklist of things the team should look at today (reviews, approvals, sends).
- Walk the list, check off what's handled, add anything missing.

### 4. To-dos (8 min)
- The shared action-item list (same list as the weekly L10 — completing one here updates the weekly).
- Scan for items **due today or overdue** — these are visually flagged.
- **Complete** what's done; **add** new to-dos with an assignee, due date, and priority.
- Keep it tight — a to-do is a <1-week, single-owner commitment. Bigger work belongs to a Rock (weekly).

---

## Rules of the road
- **30 minutes, hard stop.** If a topic needs more than ~60 seconds of debate, it's an **Issue** → send it to the weekly L10 IDS, don't solve it live.
- **One owner per task.** No shared accountability.
- **Update the board live**, in the meeting — the board *is* the record. No separate notes.
- **Slips get named, not hidden.** Saying "this slipped" early is the point of a daily.

## Definitions
- **Headline** — a client's short daily update; its bullets are the day's tasks.
- **Responsible** — the single owner of a bullet/to-do.
- **To-do** — a short (<1 week), single-owner commitment. Shared with the weekly L10.
- **Issue (IDS)** — anything needing real discussion; parked for the weekly L10.
