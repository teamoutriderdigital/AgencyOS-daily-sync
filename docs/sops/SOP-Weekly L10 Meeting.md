# SOP — Weekly L10 Meeting

**Purpose:** The weekly leadership/team meeting (EOS "Level 10"). Same-time, same-agenda every week. It reviews the quarter's Rocks, closes the loop on the week's client work and to-dos, and — most importantly — **identifies, discusses, and solves** the issues that actually move the business.

**Cadence:** Weekly, same day + time
**Time-box:** 60 minutes (start and end on time)
**Attendees:** Core team (Daniel, Jack, Leo, Rehan, Kas, Rasika; Darko as needed)
**Facilitator:** Fixed owner runs the agenda + time; a second person keeps the board updated live
**Board:** `…/weekly` (ISO-week selector; "Sync previous week items" rolls open to-dos/issues forward)

---

## Agenda (60 min)

| # | Section | Time | Goal |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Completed since last meeting | 3 min | Momentum — what got closed this week |
| 2 | Client headlines | 8 min | Per-client status; mark done; reassign; flag slips |
| 3 | IDS (Identify · Discuss · Solve) | 25 min | Actually solve the issues — the core of the meeting |
| 4 | To-dos | 5 min | Review last week's; capture new from IDS |
| 5 | Backlog (≈ twice a month) | 4 min | Triage future-client / parked ideas |
| 6 | Rocks | 10 min | Per-person quarterly Rock review + status |
| 7 | Innovation | 3 min | New tools / discoveries worth sharing |
| 8 | Meeting rating | 2 min | Everyone rates the meeting 1–10 |

*(Reorder is fine, but keep IDS as the biggest block and the rating last.)*

---

## How to run each section

### 1. Completed since last meeting (3 min)
- Auto-listed: every Rock, issue, and to-do **closed this week**, grouped by department (Admin / Growth / Internal).
- Read it out as a quick win lap. No discussion — it's for momentum.

### 2. Client headlines (8 min)
- One entry per client: **client · stage · lead**, then bullet tasks with **owners** and **done boxes** (mirrored from the latest daily; fully editable here).
- Owner gives a 15–20s update per client. **Tick off** what's done, **reassign** owners, **add tasks** as needed.
- A client problem that needs solving → **drop it into IDS**, don't solve it in the headline.

### 3. IDS — Identify, Discuss, Solve (25 min)  ⟵ the point of the meeting
- The issues list is your agenda. Each issue has an **owner, status, priority, department**, and can be **linked to a Rock**.
- **Prioritize:** use **upvotes** to rank — solve the most-voted first. Don't go top-to-bottom by default.
- For each issue, run the loop:
  1. **Identify** — state the *real* issue in one sentence (the root, not the symptom).
  2. **Discuss** — only as long as it takes to reach a decision. Facilitator cuts tangents.
  3. **Solve** — land a **decision + a to-do with an owner and a due date**. Mark the issue **Solved** (it archives and drops off both boards).
- Big/strategic issues → make it (or link it to) a **Rock**, don't try to solve a quarter-sized problem in 5 minutes.
- Aim to fully solve the top few issues, not to "touch" all of them.

### 4. To-dos (5 min)
- Review last week's to-dos: **done or not?** (~90% should be done.)
- Not done → decide: keep, re-own, or convert to an Issue.
- Capture every **new to-do** that came out of IDS — single owner, due date, priority.

### 5. Backlog (≈ twice a month, 4 min)
- Future-client ideas and parked items. Default view shows **unreviewed** only.
- Triage: promote to a to-do/Rock, keep, or delete. Mark **reviewed** as you go.

### 6. Rocks (10 min)
- The quarter's Rocks, **collapsed per person** (Jack, Daniel, Leo, Rehan, Kas). Each toggle shows the person's Rock count and **% On Track**.
- Expand each person; for every Rock set status: **On track / Off track / Done**.
- "On track" = anything not Off track (a Done Rock is a win, not a risk). The section shows the **team on-track %**.
- **Off track** is not a discussion here — it becomes an **Issue** for next week's IDS (or this week's if there's time).
- Each Rock card shows its **department** and **progress note** (e.g. 3/6).

### 7. Innovation (3 min)
- Log new tools, workflows, or discoveries worth adopting (title + link + who found it). One line each; adopt-decisions become to-dos/Rocks.

### 8. Meeting rating (2 min)
- Everyone drops a **1–10** on the meeting. Anything below ~8 gets one sentence on *why*, so the meeting improves. End on time.

---

## Rules of the road
- **Start and end on time.** The rating keeps everyone honest.
- **IDS is for solving, not reporting.** If it's a status update, it belongs in Headlines/Rocks, not IDS.
- **Every issue closes with a decision + owned to-do.** "Let's think about it" is not a solve.
- **One owner per Rock / to-do / issue.** No co-ownership.
- **Off-track Rocks become Issues**, not live debates.
- **The board is the record** — update it live; no parallel minutes.
- **Prioritize by votes**, not by list order — protect time for what matters most.

## Definitions
- **Rock** — a quarterly priority with one owner and a clear "done." Reviewed weekly, not re-debated.
- **Issue (IDS)** — anything blocking progress or needing a decision. Identify → Discuss → Solve.
- **To-do** — a <1-week, single-owner commitment coming out of an issue.
- **Department** — Admin / Growth / Internal, used to group Rocks, issues, and to-dos.
- **Headline** — a client's short update; its bullets are tasks with owners + done state.
- **On track %** — share of a person's (or the team's) Rocks that are *not* Off track.
